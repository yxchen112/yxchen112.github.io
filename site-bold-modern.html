M.S. Candidate in Power Engineering and Engineering Thermophysics, Tianjin University

Yuxuan Chen is a third-year M.S. student in Power Engineering and Engineering Thermophysics at Tianjin University, advised by Prof. Hongna Zhang. The work starts from a practical gap in viscoelastic turbulence: experiments can measure velocity, but not the polymer stress that drives the flow. PhySF-UNO, published in Physics of Fluids, reconstructs the full conformation tensor from velocity with a Fourier-operator U-Net and a positive-definiteness constraint. FAMIR, the follow-up project, uses the future state available during training to make long recursive flow forecasts drift less, tested on viscoelastic channel flow, Rayleigh–Bénard convection and rotating shallow water. Before this, Yuxuan studied engineering mechanics at Tianjin University and spent a summer at the AI for Science Institute, Beijing, building generative models for centrifugal sprays. Yuxuan is applying for PhD programs starting in Fall 2027.
